About Us

The AYN Story

Who We Are

A Multidisciplinary Design, Fabric & Engineering Collective

AYN is a multidisciplinary design, fabric, and engineering collective. Through our three specialized subsidiaries—AYN100, AYN FABRICS, and AM FORTIS ENGG—we provide comprehensive, turnkey solutions ranging from custom interior styling to clinical laboratory engineering.

Driven by innovation and backed by parental brand equity, we engineer spaces that inspire. Our structure enables clients to select and procure high-end bespoke furniture finishes, premium textiles, or scientific-grade cleanroom installations under a single, highly dependable corporate entity.

We employ modern design tools, state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, and stringent quality control protocols to listen to client expectations and deliver projects across the UAE & India that are both aesthetically perfect and functionally robust.

Our Foundation

Core Values

At AYN Group, we aim higher — working to engineer better functional spaces every single day

Environmental Responsibility

Manufacturing products that are durable, design-centric, and sustainable — with a commitment to positive ecological impact across our wood, textile, and steel supply lines.

Social Accountability

Commitment to fair labor practices and safe working environments across all manufacturing plants, ensuring positive community impact as good global corporate citizens.

Economic Fairness

Providing high-precision engineering and bespoke styling services at highly competitive prices relative to the premium materials and craftsmanship we deploy.

Uncompromising Quality

From fine textile stitches to reinforced clinical joints, every item manufactured by AYN100, AYN Fabrics, and AM Fortis Engg undergoes strict laboratory-standard testing.

Engineering & Innovation

Continuously pushing technological boundaries, blending traditional artisanal craftsmanship with state-of-the-art automation and medical-grade materials science.

Customer Peace of Mind

Creating a streamlined, stress-free customer journey. We handle everything from space planning and drafting to transport, installation, and certification.

How We Work

Our Process

01

Consultation & Planning

Our specialists collaborate with your team to detail specifications, draft space plans, and match aesthetic desires with functional rules.

02

Material Curation

Choose from our extensive global fabric libraries, premium solid woods, or advanced sterile chemical-resistant surfaces tailored to your project.

03

Precision Engineering

Our division craftsmen utilize computerized design layouts and advanced machinery to fabricate custom wood, textiles, or laboratory steel.

04

Quality Verification

Every piece is vetted for structural strength, stitch perfection, chemical compliance, and dimensions before leaving our corporate plants.

05
