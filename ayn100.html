AYN100 FURNITURE

Brand Positioning

Bespoke Craftsmanship for Distinctive Spaces

AYN100 is where your vision meets world-class artisanal execution. We specialize in high-end, fully customized furniture tailored to the exact aesthetic and functional requirements of luxury residential, commercial, and hospitality projects.

Utilizing premium materials and clean geometric design philosophies, we transform concepts into timeless, durable statement pieces. We partner with interior designers, architects, and owners to deliver bespoke woodwork and fit-outs that define luxury.

What We Do

Core Offerings

Custom Luxury Residential

Tailored sofas, premium dining tables, custom bed frames, and bespoke cabinetry for living rooms, bedrooms, and dining halls that reflect personal taste.

Bespoke Office & Commercial

Executive desks, boardroom tables, customized workstations, reception counters, and corporate seating built for high-end professional spaces.

Hospitality & Retail Concepts

Heavy-duty, premium-finish hotel suite furniture, lounge seating, restaurant fit-outs, and bespoke store concept furniture designed for hospitality standards.

Turnkey Space Planning & Material Selection

Comprehensive design consulting, layout optimization, and material matching to ensure a cohesive luxury statement throughout your entire floor plan.
