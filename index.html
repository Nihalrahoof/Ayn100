About The Group

Engineered
To Inspire

AYN is a multidisciplinary design, fabric, and engineering collective. Through our three specialized subsidiaries—AYN100, AYN FABRICS, and AM FORTIS ENGG—we provide comprehensive, turnkey solutions ranging from custom interior styling to clinical laboratory engineering. Driven by innovation and backed by parental brand equity, we engineer spaces that inspire.

Whether it is luxury customized residential spaces, soft furnishings curation, or sterile clinical labs, our collective brings a decade of regional delivery expertise, absolute precision, and dependability to every space we build.
