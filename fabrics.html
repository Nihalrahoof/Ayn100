AYN FABRICS

Brand Positioning

Premium Textiles & Window Dressings for Inspired Interiors

AYN FABRICS curates and manufactures an elite range of luxury textiles, custom curtains, and soft furnishings. Designed to complement high-end interiors, our collections blend tactile elegance with technical performance.

By offering sophisticated light control, acoustic enhancement, and flawless drapery, we provide designers and homeowners with the perfect canvas to soften, warm, and elevate any environment.

Core Offerings

Custom Curtains & Drapery

Bespoke curtains, sheers, and heavy drapes tailored to your windows. Includes advanced motorized and automated window treatments for smart homes.

Premium Upholstery Fabrics

An extensive library of premium residential and commercial fabrics sourced from global mills, ideal for sofas, chairs, panels, and wall coverings.

Flame-Retardant & Performance

Highly durable, flame-retardant (FR), acoustic-enhancing, and stain-resistant performance textiles ideal for large-scale hospitality and retail fit-outs.
